About this site

This site is a curated guide for students in Japan who want to explore international film, animation, and media competitions.

As a VFX compositor based in London, I noticed that many global opportunities are not easily visible from Japan. This project was created to bridge that gap.

This site is independently maintained. While I aim to keep the information accurate, eligibility and deadlines may change, so please always refer to the official websites.

I hope this helps you take the next step.
